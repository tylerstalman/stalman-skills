# Full Stack Creator brand

A short, portable summary so the skill knows the show wherever it runs. The full brand guide and artwork live in Tyler's Dropbox and are not in this public repository.

## The show

- **Full Stack Creator** is a podcast for people who make things for a living: photographers, filmmakers and other creative professionals.
- Hosts: **Tyler Stalman** and **David Altizer**, often with a guest.
- Topics: cameras, Apple hardware and software, creative tools, AI for creators, and the business of creative work.
- The podcast is supported by Full Stack Creator courses.

## Voice

Two friends who make things for a living, talking to people who want to do the same. Direct, warm, a little funny. No corporate marketing tone.

- Write the way Tyler talks. Read it aloud; if it sounds like a template, rewrite it.
- Short sentences. Specifics over adjectives. Useful before impressive.
- Avoid stock AI phrasing such as "elevate", "unlock", "seamless", "dive in", "whether you're X or Y", and lists of three for rhythm.
- Never overpromise. State what is true now and stop. No "always", "never" or "forever" about pricing, ads or availability.
- Tagline: "Make the whole thing." Support line: "Useful before impressive."
- Attitude: curious, crafted, generous, direct.

## Look

Retro workshop and silkscreen print: hand-made, ink on canvas, not slick tech.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Heritage Orange | `#D96012` | Primary accent |
| Maker Green | `#3B603E` | Secondary accent |
| Canvas Cream | `#F1EBD5` | Light background, text on dark |
| Workshop Ink | `#22231E` | Dark background, text on light |

Brand guide fonts: Bowlby One SC for headlines, Times Italic for subtitles and pull quotes, Public Sans for body.

Use the supplied logo artwork. Do not rebuild it.
