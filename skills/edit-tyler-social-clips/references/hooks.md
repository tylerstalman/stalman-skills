# Opening hooks

## Copy

- One short sentence, three to seven words, about this clip and not the whole episode.
- A question or a statement. Descriptive, accurate and explicit about the topic.
- No clickbait, exaggeration or promise the discussion does not keep.
- Keep approved copy unchanged when adjusting placement or size.

## Style

- **Staatliches**, black text on solid white rectangular strips, centred horizontally, compact line spacing.
- Uppercase styling must still preserve case-sensitive product suffixes.
- Riverside's Speaker preset is only a starting point. Inspect the actual font, colours and box effect.
- Dialogue captions keep their own approved style.

## Duration

- Start new hooks at six seconds. Hold longer if the copy needs more reading time.
- Check the whole playable hold, including shot changes, caption clearance and competing inserts.

## Placement

- **Single shot:** lower, below the face, around the chest or microphone. Keep eyes, face and captions readable. See [the single-shot reference](../assets/preferred-hook-lower-single-shot.jpg).
- **Stacked two-shot opening:** centre the hook across the seam between the two people. See [the two-shot reference](../assets/preferred-hook-two-shot-seam.jpg). Tyler likes two-shot openings, but they are not required.
- Keep intentional layouts. Do not regenerate Smart Layouts just to adjust text.
- Check every shot the hook appears over, including reactions and handoffs, and adjust placement or timing as needed.

## Size and platform clearance

- Smaller and less dominant than [the oversized example to avoid](../assets/avoid-hook-too-large-under-platform-search.jpg).
- Keep the whole text box clear of the platform header and search, the right-side action buttons and the bottom description area. Clear eyes do not mean clear platform buttons.
- No fixed size is confirmed. Choose size and position from the actual phone-size render, the copy length and the framing.
- The two preferred reference images set placement, not a font change.

## Verifying

- Check the saved placement in the current Riverside revision and the latest render at phone size. Settings alone do not prove clearance.
- Record which platform overlays were actually checked, and label any simulated or unverified view.
- A materially restyled render needs fresh publishing approval. Check scheduled posts before proposing a swap.

Earlier treatments (top placement, size 88) are in the [change history](changelog.md) and are not current.
