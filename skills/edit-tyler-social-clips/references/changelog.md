## Hook section as it stood before the 2026-11-11 rewrite

Every clip opens with one short sentence of three to seven words about that clip, not the whole episode. Questions and statements are acceptable. Be descriptive, accurate, and explicit about the topic. Avoid clickbait, exaggeration, or promises the discussion does not fulfill.

Use **Staatliches**, black text on solid white rectangular strips, centered horizontally, with compact line spacing. Preserve the approved copy when adjusting placement/size. Uppercase styling must still preserve meaningful case-sensitive product suffixes. Riverside's Speaker preset is a starting point only; inspect its actual font, colors and box effect. Dialogue captions keep their independent approved style.

Use **six seconds as the starting duration for new opening hooks**, extended from the previous five-second treatment by Tyler's November 7 correction. When applying that correction to an existing in-scope hook, measure its current effective duration and add one second; do not assume every existing hook was five seconds. Do not repeatedly add another second on later revisions. Keep it longer if the actual copy needs more reading time. Verify the entire playable hold, including shot changes, caption clearance and competing inserts, while preserving approved typography and copy.

Tyler's confirmed November 7 correction supersedes the earlier high/top placement and size-88 reference:

- **Single shot:** default to a lower position below the face, around the chest/microphone. Keep eyes, face and dialogue captions readable. Use [the preferred lower single-shot reference](../assets/preferred-hook-lower-single-shot.jpg) for placement/composition.
- **Opening stacked two-shot:** center the hook across the seam between people, as in [the preferred two-shot reference](../assets/preferred-hook-two-shot-seam.jpg). Tyler likes two-shot openings; they are not mandatory for every clip. Preserve intentional layouts rather than regenerating Smart Layouts for a text adjustment.
- **Scale and platform clearance:** make hooks smaller and less dominant than [the oversized high Markdown example to avoid](../assets/avoid-hook-too-large-under-platform-search.jpg). Keep the full text box clear of the platform header/search/top controls, right-side actions and bottom description/caption UI. Do not assume that keeping eyes clear also clears platform UI.

The two preferred screenshots establish placement/composition, **not a font change**. Retain Staatliches black-on-white unless Tyler explicitly changes typography. Choose reduced size and exact position from actual phone-size rendering, copy length and framing; no new fixed numeric default is confirmed. Check every shot during the hook and adjust placement or timing as needed, including reactions and handoffs. Preserve approved dialogue-caption legibility and the original gaze.
