# Full Stack Creator podcast clips

This skill works in any AI assistant that has Riverside tools. It names no assistant, and none of its steps depend on one. Where it says "image-generation tool" or "notify Tyler", use whatever the current assistant offers.

Read these before starting:

- [Shared short-form core](../short-form-video-core/SKILL.md): story, coherence and authenticity rules for every channel.
- [Brand](references/brand.md): who the show is, how it talks, colours, fonts and accounts.

Use this whole workflow for any short-form podcast clip, including when Tyler names a specific passage instead of asking for topics. These short-form preferences override full-episode defaults; leave full-episode and multicam skills alone. Use the current Riverside tools and their own guidance for how each operation, revision and transcript selection works, and never reuse media IDs, clip IDs, timestamps or facts from an earlier episode.

Clips from Tyler's main YouTube channel use `clip-tyler-youtube`. Do not carry this skill's branding, Riverside defaults, layouts or accounts into them.

## Rules that never bend

1. **Episode first.** No clip is published, or scheduled to go out, before the full episode is public on the Full Stack Creator YouTube channel. Check the channel itself. A scheduled, private or unlisted episode is not live.
2. **Tyler approves every post.** Publishing or scheduling needs his yes for the exact clips and the exact copy. A yes covers those clips only. Never publish or schedule as a way to stage or test.
3. **Eye Contact is always off.** No eye or gaze correction for anyone, in any edit, including inherited settings and later keyframes. Check the latest render. See [video authenticity](references/video-authenticity.md).
4. **Exact product names.** Confirm the product, generation and variant from the actual discussion, then use the manufacturer's short official name with its exact spelling and case, everywhere.
5. **Right episode.** Confirm each clip's source recording belongs to the requested episode. A shared studio or project is not proof.
6. **No duplicates.** Before posting, list existing uploads and confirm this clip is not already posted or scheduled on that platform.
7. **Live posts stay put.** A new export does not update a live post. Never silently replace, repost or delete one, and never restyle the back catalog because a preference changed.
8. **Report what was not checked.** Inspecting settings is not a review. Say plainly which checks were done by playback, which by sampling and which were not done.

## The weekly routine

Full detail is in [the weekly system](references/weekly-system.md). In short:

1. **Prepare early.** When the episode recording is in Riverside, start work while the episode is still being edited for YouTube.
2. **Fast two.** Build the two safest clips. Ask Tyler once: "post these two when the episode is live?" After his yes and once the episode is confirmed public, post them to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts within 30 to 60 minutes.
3. **Best-of batch.** Give Tyler a longer candidate list. He steers, the clips get built, he reviews and approves, and they go out at about two a day for the following week.
4. **Older episodes.** A strong clip from the back catalogue can fill a slot. The episode-first rule is already satisfied for those.
5. **Learn.** Log every pick, kill and fix note, plus view counts once posts have had a few days. Read that log before choosing topics for the next episode.

Keep each episode's progress in its status file so any assistant, on either Mac, can pick up where the last one stopped.

## Making a clip, in order

### 1. Choose topics

- Read the taste log first. Favour what Tyler has picked and what has performed; avoid what he has killed.
- Build a long, detailed list of topics. Treat each topic's beginning as a likely entry point, because it gives new viewers context.
- Prefer a clear takeaway, a surprise, useful advice, a distinctive opinion or a lively exchange. These are editorial judgments, not predictions.
- Check existing clips and uploads so a topic is not repeated.

### 2. Shape the story

- Make the subject clear in the first few spoken seconds. Trim opening filler ("so", "like", "um") without clipping real words.
- Aim for one to two minutes when the point fits. Source material for one clip can span five minutes or more. Coherence beats length: keep the setup and the bridges, and use a longer passage when shortening would break the explanation.
- Remove tangents, detours and repetition. Keep a beginning, middle, end and real takeaway, along with the speakers' meaning and qualifications.
- Let both speakers contribute when that improves the clip. Single-speaker clips are fine.

### 3. Clean up audio

For a new clip, turn on all five Riverside AI tools: Remove Pauses, Remove Filler Words, Magic Audio for every speaker, Smart Mute, and Smart Layouts with cutaways. Favour stronger cleanup. When revising an existing edit, keep Tyler's manual settings and do not rerun cleanup or regenerate layouts. Details and the crosstalk checks are in [quality checks](references/riverside-quality-checks.md).

### 4. Set the layout

9:16 vertical. Vary the opening, change angle at least a couple of times, and keep the current speaker in the largest tile. Full rules are in [layouts](references/layouts.md).

### 5. Add the opening hook

One sentence of three to seven words about this clip. Descriptive and accurate, never clickbait. Staatliches, black on white strips, held about six seconds. Placement and sizing are in [hooks](references/hooks.md).

### 6. Set captions

Use Tyler's saved Riverside favourite: bold white Montserrat, word by word, dark shadow. Captions and hooks keep separate styles. Adding a graphic never changes the captions. See [review and publishing](references/review-and-publishing.md).

### 7. Add visual inserts

- Use only real material: manufacturer photos, genuine screenshots, original footage. Check the gear reference library before sourcing anything new, and add new verified photos to it.
- Use the fixed menu: product card, screenshot on canvas, real footage. Each insert starts on the spoken reference and holds long enough to read at phone size.
- **Fast-two clips:** if there is no verified real asset for what is being discussed, add no insert and stay on faces. Do not invent artwork for abstract ideas.
- **Best-of clips:** custom animation is allowed with Tyler involved.
- Render graphics outside Riverside as one finished vertical video, then upload it.

Rules and recipes are in [visual inserts](references/visual-inserts.md).

### 8. Run the final checklist

Use the checklist below on the latest render, not on settings. The detailed glitch-cut and playback checks are in [quality checks](references/riverside-quality-checks.md).

### 9. Write the copy

Write from the show's point of view, in the voice described in [brand](references/brand.md). No hashtags. End on a statement, not a question. Generally leave host names out; use a guest's full name. Prepare the account list, titles, captions, visibility and cover frames as one package Tyler can read. See [review and publishing](references/review-and-publishing.md).

### 10. Hand off for review

Give Tyler a numbered list: each clip's title, hook, length, caption and where to watch the current version. He can answer by number, typed or dictated ("clip 3, the hook is too vague"). Record every answer in the taste log.

## Final checklist

Do every item on the current render. Mark each as checked by full playback, checked by sampling, or not checked.

- [ ] Episode is public on YouTube (or this clip is held until it is)
- [ ] Source recording belongs to the right episode
- [ ] Eye Contact off for every participant, and eyes look natural in playback
- [ ] Product names and models verified and spelled the official way
- [ ] Subject is clear in the first few seconds
- [ ] Whole clip read start to finish as a first-time viewer: it makes sense and ends on the real conclusion
- [ ] Hook is accurate, three to seven words, clear of faces, captions and platform buttons at phone size
- [ ] Current speaker is in the large tile through every handoff
- [ ] No flashes, few-frame shots or stray frames, especially after inserts
- [ ] No clipped words, bad joins or cuts through another speaker's words
- [ ] Every insert is real, matches what is being said and holds long enough to read
- [ ] Captions unchanged and readable
- [ ] Copy has no hashtags, no closing question and no overclaim
- [ ] Not already posted or scheduled on that platform
- [ ] Tyler has approved these exact clips and this exact copy

## Open questions

These are not settled. Do not treat any of them as a rule, and ask Tyler when one affects the work.

- **Brand mismatch.** Hooks use Staatliches black on white, screenshot canvases use `#F4F0E6` and product cards use dark forest green. The brand guide says Bowlby One SC, Canvas Cream `#F1EBD5` and Maker Green `#3B603E`. Clips stay as they are until Tyler decides.
- **Cleanup strength.** Tyler approved "stronger" cleanup without naming a setting.
- **Hook size.** No fixed number is confirmed. Size it from the phone-size render.
- **Caption preset.** Confirm in the editor that the saved preset is the favourite in the reference image.
- **Preview method.** Posting unlisted to YouTube for review is untested.
- **Native posting.** Adding captions and hooks inside TikTok on an iPhone is an untested experiment.
