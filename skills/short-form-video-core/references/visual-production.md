# Authentic visuals across channels

This file defines behavior, not a brand. Obtain colors, fonts, backgrounds, caption geometry, logos and motion character from the selected channel's approved profile. Never use the podcast's green paper, off-white canvas or typography as a fallback for the main channel. A matching technical technique can transfer without its appearance.

Use real manufacturer photos/3D models, genuine relevant app screenshots, or original footage. Preserve hardware geometry, labeling and UI; do not synthesize product pixels or missing screen content. Track source page, actual asset, verified product/version, supported claim and any compositing. A real image can still mislead when its size/generation or comparison does not match the speech. Check the depicted models and comparison basis, not only whether a source looks official.

For each insert record its spoken trigger, what it adds, source verification, entrance, readable hold, exit and adjacent shots. Fit the content to the available speech: simplify or split dense specs rather than shorten the hold until unreadable. Count reading time after the final reveal/movement settles. Use a measured caption/platform safe area where the channel calls for clearance; some channel treatments intentionally allow captions over screenshots.

For the opening, find the earliest relevant spoken cue for available visuals and check whether moving the first insert forward makes the first five seconds more interesting without losing context. Five seconds is an attention priority, not a mandatory shot length or permission to invent coverage.

Confirmed shared feedback, November 7, 2026: Tyler asked to cut to available visuals as soon as possible and make the first five seconds as interesting as possible. Apply this general timing lesson across short-form clips; keep individual reel revisions and publishing decisions in their jobs.

Reusable motion mechanisms include a brief screenshot entrance settling to a still view, a product cutout/spec card, and a restrained official-model pose/hinge entrance followed by a hold. Bind them to channel style tokens. They are options, not an insert quota or a ban on other purposeful motion. Preserve the approved appearance of an existing edit. Do not add hardware effects, glowing logos or feature demonstrations unless verified for that exact product. Screens must show genuine relevant software; mark composite screens in provenance.

Keep caption/hook/spec styles separate. Render product-name suffixes with a font that preserves their meaningful case. Verify the photo, readable hold and actual layers in the delivered video; a timeline/upload record is insufficient. Inspect both sides of overlay boundaries for brief exposed underlying shots. In Riverside, also verify the current native Clips feed when that is the review destination. In Final Cut/Resolve, verify saved native timeline, linked media, output color and the current export.
